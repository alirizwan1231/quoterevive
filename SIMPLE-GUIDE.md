# QuoteRevive — Simple Guide (Finn ke liye)

## 1. Ye hai kia?

- **Contractor** matlab wo mistri/company wala jo gharon ka kaam karta hai — kitchen banana, chhat (roof) theek karna, AC lagana, paint karna.
- Kaam se pehle wo customer ko aik **quote** deta hai — matlab rate/estimate: "aap ka kaam $5,000 me ho jayega."
- Masla ye hai ke aksar customer jawab hi nahi deta. Wo quote waisi ki waisi pari rehti hai — is ko hum **dead quote** kehte hain.
- **QuoteRevive unhi dead quotes ko dobara zinda karne wala tool hai** — taake soya hua kaam wapas mil jaye.

## 2. Masla kia hai? (aik chhoti si example)

Aik contractor mahine me **40 quotes** bhejta hai.

- Un me se aadhi, yani **20 quotes**, ka koi jawab nahi ata.
- Har quote average **$5,000** ki ho, to 20 × $5,000 = **$100,000 ka kaam** har mahine chup chaap soya para hai.
- Contractor busy hota hai, wo follow-up (dobara poochna) bhool jata hai. Kaam kisi aur ko mil jata hai, ya reh jata hai.

Bas yehi masla QuoteRevive hal karta hai.

## 3. Tool kaam kese karta hai? (5 simple steps)

1. **Account banao** — contractor apna naam, company ka naam aur email de ke free account bana leta hai.
2. **Purani quotes ki list upload karo** — aik **CSV file** upload hoti hai. CSV matlab aik simple si file jo Excel ya Jobber (contractors ka software) se banti hai, us me customer ka naam, email aur quote ki raqam hoti hai.
3. **Tool 4 emails ka plan banata hai** — Day 0, phir Day 3, phir Day 10, phir Day 25 pe aik aik narmi wali yaad-dihani email. Email ka text pehle se likha hua milta hai, badal bhi sakte hain.
4. **Apne Gmail se aik click pe bhejo** — email contractor ke **apne Gmail** se jati hai (hamare pas se nahi), to customer ko asal banda hi lagta hai. Tool me "Open in Gmail" ka button dabao, email ready milti hai, bas Send karna hota hai.
5. **Reply aye to baaki emails khud ruk jati hain** — customer jawab de de to aage wali emails cancel ho jati hain. Agar kaam mil jaye, to wo raqam **dashboard** pe "Revived $" me nazar ati hai. (Dashboard matlab wo screen jahan saari cheezein aik nazar me dikhti hain — kitni quotes hain, kitni emails gayin, kitna paisa wapas aya.)

## 4. Paisay kese bane ge?

- **Abhi testing chal rahi hai: har user ko 1 month bilkul FREE hai.** Koi card nahi, koi paisa nahi.
- Us ke baad har contractor **$10/month** dega.
- Simple hisaab (ye sirf math ki example hai, koi wada nahi):
  - 10 customer = **$100/month**
  - 50 customer = **$500/month**
  - 100 customer = **$1,000/month**
- **Tumhara kharcha abhi $0 hai** — site Vercel pe free chal rahi hai aur data wala system (Supabase) bhi free wala hai.

## 5. Abhi kahan khare hain?

- Site **live hai**: quoterevive-cyan.vercel.app — koi bhi ja ke account bana sakta hai.
- Accounts asal hain: contractor kisi bhi phone/computer se login kar sakta hai, us ka data **Supabase** me save hota hai. (Supabase matlab aik online almari — data tumhare browser me nahi, mehfooz jagah pe rehta hai.)
- **48 contractors ki list tayyar hai** (America, Australia, UK se) jin ko message karna hai. Un ke liye DM aur email ke drafts bhi likhe hue hain.
- **Abhi tak kisi ko kuch bheja nahi gaya** — bhejna sirf tumhare kehne pe hoga.

## 6. Tum ko kia karna hai?

Sirf 3 cheezein:

1. **Apni pasand batani hai** — design ya product me koi cheez pasand na aye to kehna hai, wo badal jayegi.
2. **Drafts parh ke ijazat deni hai** — contractors ko bhejne wale messages pehle tum parhoge, phir hi jayenge.
3. **Reply aye to aage madad** — jab koi contractor jawab de, to aage ki baat cheet/decide karne me tumhara mashwara chahiye hoga.

Baaki saara kaam — banana, test karna, list nikalna, drafts likhna — ho raha hai.
