## How each entry was verified

- Every business was found via web search and confirmed as a real, currently operating company with its own website.
- Every email was read on the business's **own website** (homepage, services page or contact page — see the `source_url` column for the exact page). No email was invented, guessed, or taken from a third-party directory.
- Instagram handles are included only where the business's own site linked them (7 of 48); contact-page URLs are included where that page was opened.
- Two entries carry a small caveat: Greensward Group (Charlotte, NC) and Beyond Heating and Cooling (Melbourne) — their emails were confirmed from their own sites' indexed text after direct page fetches were rate-limited/masked. A 10-second re-check of their contact pages before first outreach is sensible.
- Two candidates were dropped during research because their sites blocked verification (a Dallas roofing firm and a Perth renovation firm) and were replaced with fully verified businesses.
